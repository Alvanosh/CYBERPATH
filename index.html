How it works

A simple four-step path from learning to readiness.

01

Choose your target role.

Map your goals to a security specialty that matches your interests and strengths.

02

Follow the learning roadmap.

Move through structured stages with prerequisite-based sequencing and hands-on practice.

03

Practise in authorized labs.

Use safe, legal lab environments and build real-world projects that strengthen your portfolio.

04

Track progress and prepare for work.

Measure growth, capture evidence of skills, and build readiness for internships or jobs.
